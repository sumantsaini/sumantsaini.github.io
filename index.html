Who Am I?

Somewhere between a fantasy RPG and a questionable life decision, a legend was born. Known across imaginary kingdoms as the Imperialistic (yes that is my gamer tag), I spend my time playing video games, collecting beautiful gems, practicing archery, and attempting to survive coding assignments.

I may not have a legendary sword or a million gold coins, but I have a bow, a growing collection of shiny rocks, and a strong determination to close my code editor whenever things go wrong.

My Origin Story

I started of as a boring accounting major, after working in industry, I quickly found out it wasn't for me, Than I took the MDM program, and found my love of creating video games, and now I intent to start a video game studio after this degree.

Unfortunately, my journey also introduced me to coding. I was promised creativity and technology. Nobody warned me about missing semicolons, mysterious errors, and spending an hour searching for one tiny mistake.

Now I continue my quest: mastering new skills, discovering rare treasures, defeating virtual enemies, and hopefully convincing my computer to cooperate.
