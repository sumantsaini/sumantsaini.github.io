Three Facts the Public Must Know

Fact #1: The Gaming Addiction

Give me a good video game and suddenly time becomes a fictional concept. I might start with one quest and somehow end up exploring an entire world without noticing how late it is.

Fact #2: Shiny Objects Are a Serious Distraction

Gems and minerals have a special place in my heart. A rare, colourful crystal can be more exciting than finding legendary loot in a video game. My imaginary treasure chest is always accepting new additions.

Fact #3: Coding Is My Ultimate Nemesis

I can face virtual bosses and practice archery, but one mysterious coding error can test my patience like nothing else. The code works perfectly until someone asks me to demonstrate it.

Survival Guide: Working With Me

Follow these steps carefully if you wish to survive a group project, gaming session, or coding assignment with the subject.

  • Explain the mission clearly. Confusing instructions may trigger unnecessary side quests.
  • Allow time for gaming discussions. A quick conversation about a favourite game may become a full review.
  • Never interrupt an important gem discovery. The treasure must be inspected properly.
  • If coding becomes frustrating, remain calm and help investigate the problem instead of making random changes.
  • Be respectful to Buddy, the Labrador. He is an important member of the team and may demand attention without warning.
  • Celebrate small victories. Getting the code to work is a legendary achievement, even if it took twenty attempts.

Known Weaknesses

  • Coding errors-A missing character can turn a peaceful afternoon into a boss battle.
  • Rare gems-An irresistible weakness for beautiful minerals and crystals.
  • Video games-The phrase "just one more round" has been responsible for many lost hours.
  • Buddy's puppy eyes-Difficult to resist, even when he is clearly asking for something he does not need.
  • Overconfidence-Occasionally believing a task will be easy before discovering seventeen unexpected problems.

Future Ambitions

The subject has several objectives for the future, some realistic and others suspiciously ambitious.

  • Become more confident with coding and eventually defeat the dreaded debugging boss.
  • Continue improving at archery and develop greater accuracy and control.
  • Expand the gem collection with interesting and unique finds.
  • Explore more games, stories, and creative worlds.
  • Keep learning new technology and use it to create interesting projects.
  • Give Buddy plenty of adventures, treats, and happy memories.
